Privacy

Last updated 22 November 2026

This page describes this website. It is plain-language information, not legal advice.

What this site stores in your browser

No cookies. If you use the theme or motion buttons in the footer, your choice is saved in your browser’s local storage under gm-theme or gm-motion. It never leaves your device, and you can clear it at any time.

Fonts and third parties

Fonts are served from this site, so your browser makes no request to a font provider. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may record technical request data, such as your IP address, under its own privacy policy.

Visit counts

If enabled, the site uses GoatCounter for simple page-view counts. GoatCounter states that it does not use cookies. The footer says whether counts are currently on.

If you contact us

Email and telephone links open your own app. The interest form either opens a pre-filled email to udoudomharvey@gmail.com or, if a form service is configured, sends your name, email, role and message to that service and to us. We use it only to reply to you, and you can ask us to delete it.

About Aura-M1

The product is separate from this website. In the prototype, camera video is processed in memory on the user’s device and is not saved or uploaded, and the local dashboard listens only on that machine. Optional voice wording sends a text label only, never video or body data. Aura-M1 is an ergonomic and focus assistant, not a medical device.
