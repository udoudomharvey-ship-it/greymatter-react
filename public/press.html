Press kit

Everything needed to describe GreyMatter accurately

In one paragraph

GreyMatter is a founder-led technology company building Aura-M1, a private, on-device focus assistant. Using only an ordinary webcam, Aura-M1 notices when focus is slipping and suggests one short, explained reset. It is an early prototype: detection accuracy has not yet been measured, and validation on labelled sessions is the next step.

Facts

  • Company: GreyMatter. Tagline: “Where intelligence begins.”
  • Product: Aura-M1, early prototype.
  • Chief Executive Officer: Harvey Udoudom.
  • Origin: research begun at the University of Uyo in 2025, developed through the iDICE Startup Bridge Founders Lab.
  • Privacy: video is processed in memory on the device and is never saved or uploaded.

Founder

Harvey Udoudom is the founder and Chief Executive Officer of GreyMatter and the author of the Aura-M1 prototype. LinkedIn

Please do not say

  • That Aura-M1 is a medical device, or that it diagnoses anything.
  • Any accuracy figure. None has been measured.
  • That the low-power hardware figures are results. They are modelled targets.
  • That it has pilot customers. It has none yet.
